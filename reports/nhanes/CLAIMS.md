## Wording the results do not fully support

1. **"early CKD" next to 0.86.** The 0.86 is for all-stage CKD (eGFR < 60 OR ACR >= 30) and eGFR is
   both a feature and half of that label, so part of 0.86 is definitional. Measured early-stage
   performance (test participants with eGFR >= 60, where CKD can only be albuminuria) is
   **0.75 AUC [0.73, 0.77]**: real signal, well above the eGFR-only baseline (0.56), but
   moderate. "Early" is justified only with 0.75 next to it; 0.86 as an "early CKD" result overstates.
2. **"0.95 AUC on 399 hospital patients."** This is the v3 transfer model after a disclosed second look at UCI: the first-look v2 result was 0.64. Both must appear in any write-up; the line may quote 0.95 only if the project materials state that the external set was revisited once after a pre-specified fix of a diagnosed preprocessing flaw (no tuning on UCI). It is also a 14-feature model, not the 0.86 model.
3. **"self-built kidney scores."** eGFR and the BUN:creatinine ratio are standard clinical formulas that
   you implemented and validated, not scores you invented. "Derived" or "computed" is accurate.
4. **"routine blood tests"** is supported: all 17 analytes come from a metabolic panel, a CBC and an
   HbA1c. "17 blood tests" is defensible (17 distinct analytes); "3 routine blood panels" is more conservative.
5. **"39K adults"**: say "39K US adults" if space allows; the data are a US national survey.
